# Confirm work status

Use `update_status` only when the user explicitly states or confirms the status. Include their
reason when supplied. Do not derive RESOLVED, OPEN, or any other status solely from digital
evidence. If the confirmed outcome answers an outstanding request, use the evidence to identify
the requester and prepare a separate response draft; resolving the item does not mean the requester
has been informed.
